## Refund rule

- A bad report reported within **14 days** of the report run date is replaced or refunded. A bad report is one with wrong findings, for example a check that contradicts the fetched page.
- Replacement or refund is the seller's choice.
- Refunds are for bad reports, not for a prospect declining your proposal.
- Reply within **3 business days** of receiving the request. Only promise a time that will be kept.
- Contact: **murugan@drvelu.com**. A buyer making a refund request must include the report URL, the finding they say is wrong, and why.
- Do not take payment until the refund channel (contact email in the footer, /terms, FAQ, and User-Agent, plus the mailto refund route) is live and tested. That channel is being built in this fixes round; its live status is to be confirmed by the coordinator after verification.

## How to use this log

Add one row per refund request, on the day it arrives. Record the outcome and the refund transaction ID when the refund is issued. This log is the substantiation file if a chargeback or complaint ever asks whether a refund promise was honoured. Keep it current; do not back-fill it from memory.
