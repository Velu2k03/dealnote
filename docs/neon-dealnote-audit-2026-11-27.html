What passed the honesty & compliance sweep

Before tearing down the blockers, credit where it is due: DealNote v1 avoids the compliance landmines that sink most hackathon launches. Every date, sample label, and disclaimer checked out clean on live inspection.

  • PASS No future dates: Every visible timestamp on /, /audit, /sample, and /terms reads 27 Nov 2026, matching the live audit date.
  • PASS No unsubstantiated "verified" claims: The homepage explicitly states This is DealNote's scoring model, not an industry standard and lists what it does not check (no traffic/ranking estimates, no Google Maps/Places data, no legal accessibility claims).
  • PASS No invented fill rates or fake case studies: Harbor & Pine Plumbing on / and /sample is repeatedly marked SAMPLE: fictional business, fictional data across the banner, score card, findings, investment table, and footer.
  • PASS Earnings disclaimer sits next to the price claim: Right inside the / pricing block: $49 once is less than two months of the cheapest white-label audit subscription at $39 per month. Arithmetic on published prices, not an earnings claim. No guarantee of client wins.
  • PASS Clean outreach boundary & sticky nav: /templates explicitly states Email only. No cold SMS scripts in this product with an outreach-law disclaimer. The sticky top header remains pinned smoothly across all five live routes.
