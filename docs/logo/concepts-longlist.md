# Concepts Longlist & Selection — DealNote

> **Selection Decision:** **Concepts 1, 2, and 4** were chosen to build first in Phase 4 (`concept-a-notched-note.svg`, `concept-b-capital-d-folio.svg`, `concept-c-clipped-index-tab.svg`).

## Neon's Ten One-Sentence Concepts (Verbatim)

1. **Notched Note (Teardown Corner):** crisp geometric vertical rectangle with a sharp 45-degree diagonal slice cleanly detached from the top-right corner, reading as a torn-off note corner and a mark of precision.
2. **Capital D Folio:** bold geometric D whose vertical stem is slightly offset from the curved bowl by a razor-thin negative-space gap, reading simultaneously as the D in DealNote and an open two-page proposal.
3. **Redline Margin Mark:** solid square block bisected vertically by a clean left margin rule and a single short horizontal strike, evoking an editor's redline pass on a printed page.
4. **Clipped Index Tab:** sharp horizontal index-card silhouette with a top-left file tab and a single crisp vertical cut through the middle, suggesting a freelancer's organised desk file at a glance.
5. **Negative-Space DN Monolith:** solid geometric D enclosing a sharp diagonal stroke that forms an implicit N in negative space, delivering an unmistakable monogram in two simple shapes.
6. **Torn-Edge Sheet:** minimalist rectangular note whose bottom edge ends in two sharp geometric teeth (not a soft wave), suggesting a page torn from a pad after a teardown.
7. **Corner Bracket Scope:** two opposing 90-degree viewfinder brackets framing a single solid tilted slash, evoking a precise audit scope and a fast, decisive cut.
8. **Stacked Two-Sheet Offset:** two crisp overlapping rectangles offset at a tight 15-degree or stepped-grid angle, representing the audit report plus the ready-to-send proposal as one pair.
9. **Ink Stamp D:** heavy, slightly squared-off geometric D with a sharp internal diagonal wedge cut into the counter, reading like an editorial grade stamp on paper.
10. **Folded Ribbon N:** three crisp parallel geometric strokes forming a tight N whose center diagonal doubles as a sharp bookmark crease, suggesting speed and precision.

### Why Concepts 1, 2, and 4 Were Selected First

- **Maximum strategic range across mark types:**
  - **Concept 1 (`Notched Note`)** directly subverts the clichéd dog-ear paper page: instead of a folded flap + 3 horizontal lines + checkmark inside a tile, a single solid vertical sheet has its top-right corner cleanly sliced away at 45° and separated by a crisp void — expressing *teardown + note + precision* in two primitives.
  - **Concept 2 (`Capital D Folio`)** anchors the unique brand initial `D` via architectural negative space: an unjoined vertical stem/folio spine offset from a bold curved bowl creates a dual reading of `D` and an open two-page proposal without any container box.
  - **Concept 4 (`Clipped Index Tab`)** grounds the mark in a freelancer's physical desk ("paper, ink, organised desk file"): a crisp horizontal index-card silhouette with a top-left file tab bisected cleanly by one vertical cut — instantly legible at `16px` and zero overlap with software dashboard/doc icons.
- **24×24 integer grid compatibility:** All three rely on large, bold solid silhouettes and wide negative cuts (2–3 grid units wide) rather than thin internal counters (`DN Monolith`) or small zigzags (`Torn-Edge Sheet`), giving every selected mark the strongest chance of holding crisp edges at a `16px` favicon.
